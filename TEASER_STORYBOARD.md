# 《如果你来过》: teaser storyboard (1:31)

A teaser for the full song by realsimson. It's painted in the same p5.brush watercolor-and-ink style as the P(doom) video, with the same Clawd and the same Researcher, but it's a new story. Audio: [`assets/ruguo_teaser.mp3`](assets/ruguo_teaser.mp3).

## The idea

A young researcher who lives alone can't sleep. At 3 a.m. she opens a generative AI and types one question: 「如果有一个人，是我最想遇见的样子……他会是什么样？」 Her words paint him into the room, stroke by stroke. Everything they share afterwards is painted the same way: the umbrella in the rain, one pair of earphones, fireworks, a sunset coast. In verse 2 the paintings meet her real room. He remembers the coast, but she has never been there. In the final chorus the paint starts to run. She decides to end it. As the screen goes dark she whispers 「换你来找我」. After a long silence he answers 「好。」, and we never learn whether he generated that answer or chose it.

**Two kinds of picture.** This is the main visual idea, and it carries the twist without any text.

- **Real life** (her apartment) is dark and sparse: night indigo, cold screen teal and rain on the window. It uses few colors, slow camera moves and still, settled paint.
- **Imagination** (everything with him) is painted while we watch. Ink lines draw themselves, washes bloom in, and the edges stay loose with bare paper around the scene, like a page in a sketchbook. It's warm: rose, ochre, sky blue and sunset coral.

The audience learns to read "imagined" from how a scene is painted. The reveal is the two kinds of picture colliding. The rule that makes it work: **he is only ever life-size inside imagination paint.** In the real room he exists only on the laptop screen.

**Rule for every shot** (same as P(doom)): something *happens* on screen. A character acts, or something is drawn, washes in, runs or fades.

**Keep it text-light.** The karaoke carries the lyrics. The only other writing is her typed question at the start and the title card at the end. There are no labels, captions or sound-effect words.

## What ties it together

- **The screen is the door.** Every trip into imagination starts in the laptop's glow, and every return ends there. In quiet moments the text cursor blinks on the beat like a heartbeat.
- **Call and response.** Verse 2 cuts on every line between his memory (warm) and her reality (dark), in identical framing (match cuts).
- **Motivated transitions.** No brush-wipe chapter cards. The action carries each cut: ink droplets, a ripple of light, walls melting into rain, a phone screen, a falling tear, colors draining away, a screen powering off.
- **Emotion morphs.** Faces never snap between moods (the same `mood()` blink-and-squash change as P(doom)).
- **Camera.** It's slower than P(doom): pushes, drifts, slow orbits and match cuts. The fast moves are saved for the final chorus. Hits land on the half-time beat, every 0.909 s (66 BPM feel; the track is 132 BPM).
- **Palette arc.** Night indigo and screen teal → warm lamp ochre as he appears → rose, ochre and rain indigo, then firework violet and gold → sunset coral against a cold blue room, cutting back and forth → running paint, with colors draining to bare paper → dark indigo → black → the title in warm ochre.
- **Karaoke shows who sings.** Chinese lyrics in a brush font, highlighted rose for her lines, Clawd's orange for his and gold for duets.

## Production notes

- **Its own page.** The teaser is `teaser.html` with its scenes in `src/teaser/` (`kit.js` holds the shared sets, props and effects; `t0`–`t5` are the chapters). It shares `core.js`, `clawd.js`, `cast.js` and `render.mjs` with P(doom) and follows [`ANIMATION_GUIDE.md`](ANIMATION_GUIDE.md). P(doom)'s stage, curtains, P(doom) meter and brush-wipe chapter cards aren't used.
- **Timing.** 132 BPM, played as a 66 BPM ballad: bar lines at 1.82 + 3.636·n s and half-time beats every 0.909 s (`barT(n)`, `hbT(n)`, `hpulse(t)` in `kit.js`). Lyric and per-character timings in `src/teaser/lyrics.js` were measured from the separated vocal track.
- **Her look.** `researcher()` has new optional settings (`hair: 'long'`, `top: 'sweater'`, `lashes`); the defaults are unchanged, so P(doom) renders as before.
- **Lyrics font.** The P(doom) fonts have no Chinese characters, so the lyrics, her typed question and the title use Ma Shan Zheng, a brush font. The karaoke highlight shows who sings: rose for her, orange for him, gold for both.
- **A p5.brush limit.** Ink strokes past about x 2800, and curved shapes past about x 2800 or y 1450, get dropped or mangled even when a camera brings them on screen. Keep them inside x < 2700 and y < 1400.
- **Rendering.** On a machine with a GPU:
  ```bash
  npm install
  node render.mjs --page=teaser.html --frames=0:91.2 --workers=4   # paint every frame into out/frames_ruguo (resumable)
  node render.mjs --page=teaser.html --encode                       # join the frames and the audio into out/ruguo.mp4
  ```
  Without a GPU, add `--lowres` to both commands for a half-resolution preview. It goes to `out/frames_ruguo_lowres`, so it never mixes with full-quality frames.
